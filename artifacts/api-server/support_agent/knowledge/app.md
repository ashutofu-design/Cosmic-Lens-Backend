# App navigation, Kundli, More tools, account

## Tabs and screens
Cosmic Lens main tabs: Home, Life Map, Ask, Future. More is the drawer (not a tab). Profile and Help & Support (Cosmic Help) are under More / Profile.

## More drawer
More includes: Panchang & Muhurat; Kundli & Charts (Planet Position / Kundli — Live; Gemstones — WhatsApp); My Library (My Reports, Profile). Other tools may open from Life Map Explore or Profile.

## COSMO ID
Public User ID is COSMO + digits (example COSMO109) on Profile. Assigned at signup; cannot be changed.

## Login and language
Login: Continue with Google. If stuck: Profile → Logout, then sign in again.
App language: Profile → English / Hinglish / Hindi. Ask chat has its own reply-language pick (separate from app UI).

## Support and website
Support email: supportcosmiclens@gmail.com. Website: https://cosmiclens.app. Cosmic Help is in-app support — not V3 Live and not kundli prediction.

## Onboarding and create kundli
First-time / unlock flows: create primary kundli with name, date of birth, birth time, and place. Without kundli, many Home tools show demo or unlock banners.

## Profile and family
Kundli / birth details: Profile → edit. Add family or partner profiles (name, gender, DOB, time, place). Set one as primary. Delete account: Profile → About → type DELETE.

## Delete account effects
Deleting is permanent: login removed; kundlis, profiles, and chat erased within about 30 days. GST invoices may be kept for legal periods. Confirm by typing DELETE.

## Referral
Refer & Earn: Profile. Share code like CL + your user id. Friend enters once at signup. When that friend buys any Ask V1 or V3 pack, you get 3 free Ask questions (one reward per referred buyer).

## Home features
Home (needs kundli): Today’s Energy, 7-day Energy Forecast, Dosh Analysis, Risk Radar, Lucky Elements, Personalization. Kundli Milan may also be linked from Home.

## Personalization
Home → Personalization (needs kundli). Shows how your kundli understands you: Kundli Category (power type) with VIEW detail, Strongest Trait, Pressure Point, and scored insight cards. Free on-screen — not a PDF. Without kundli, create one first.

## Life Map
Life Map: Relationship, Career, Health, Finance. Explore: Numerology, AstroVastu, Palmistry, Face Reading (coming soon). Business Vastu from Explore / Business entry. Career from Life Map may require a ₹1 Career Analysis Unlock — see faq knowledge.

## Ask and Future
Ask: Cosmic Intelligence V1 and V3 Live (one-time packs / sessions). Future tab: on-screen current dasha timeline (MD / AD / PD style) — not a PDF.

## Kundli and charts
More → Planet Position (titled Kundli): free birth chart / planetary positions — D1, Divisional, KP, Ashtakavarga, Navatara, Jaimini, Transit tabs; planet cards show sign, degrees, house. Kundli tab also shows Ascendant, Moon, Nakshatra, and navigable dasha (MD / Antar / Pratyantar). Needs saved kundli (demo for some tabs without it).

## Panchang
More → Panchang & Muhurat. Panchang Today: Tithi, Nakshatra, Yoga, Karana, Vaar, sunrise/sunset, auspicious block, Tarabala, Brahma Muhurta, Rahu/Gulika/Yamaganda times; also Muhurat / Vrat / Vivah / Jaap tabs as shown.

## Muhurat
Muhurat: auspicious times for Wedding, Griha Pravesh, Business Start, Vehicle, Naming, Mundan, Yagyopavit, Travel. Dates are approximate — confirm with a pandit for ceremonies.

## Gemstones
More → Gemstones: Vedic gemstone guide (Navratna style tips) and certified gems shop. Request on WhatsApp for real photos/videos before paying. Prices show as “from” amounts on the product card — confirm on WhatsApp / Pay.

## Daily Alerts
Daily Alerts: daily planetary guidance cards (energy score, Moon, insight, lucky colour/numbers, and related notes). Multi-profile where shown. Push notifications may also cover V3 ready / report ready when enabled.

## Talk to Founder
Talk to Founder: Instagram / YouTube / WhatsApp links to discuss kundli or questions personally (not Cosmic Help chat).

## Divya Prashna
Divya Prashna: ask a question for an instant Vedic-style answer in that screen (separate from Ask V1 packs and Cosmic Help).

## Remedies
Remedies: planet-focused mantra, gem, daan, and upay style guidance on that screen.

## Rashifal
Rashifal: daily / weekly / monthly horoscope style reading on that screen — not Cosmic Help.

## My Reports note
Paid Pro PDFs and videos land in My Reports after delivery. See reports knowledge for ETA.
