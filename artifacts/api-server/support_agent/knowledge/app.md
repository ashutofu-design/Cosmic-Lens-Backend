# App navigation, Kundli, More tools, account

## Tabs and screens
Cosmic Lens main tabs: Home, Life Map, Ask, Future. More is the drawer (not a tab). Profile and Help & Support (Cosmic Help) are under More / Profile.

## More drawer
More includes: Panchang & Muhurat; Kundli & Charts (Planet Position / Kundli — Live; Gemstones — WhatsApp); My Library (My Reports, Profile). Other tools may open from Life Map Explore or Profile.

## COSMO ID
Public User ID is COSMO + digits (example COSMO109) on Profile. Assigned at signup; cannot be changed.

## Login — Google or phone OTP
Two login ways: (1) Continue with Google; (2) Indian mobile OTP — +91 and 10 digits, then 6-digit SMS OTP. OTP is for the app/device flow (not always on web).
If login is stuck or the app sends you back to login: Profile → Logout if you can, force-stop Cosmic Lens, open again, then sign in. Email supportcosmiclens@gmail.com if it still fails.

## OTP not received / wrong OTP
First send can take 15–30 seconds. Resend OTP unlocks after about 2 minutes. Check SMS and spam. Wrong OTP: type again or tap Resend. Session expired: tap Resend OTP and use the new code.

## App language vs Ask language vs Cosmic Help
Three different settings. Profile → language changes the whole app UI (English / Hinglish / Hindi). Ask tab has its own Hindi / English / Hinglish pick for Ask answers only. Cosmic Help replies in English or Hinglish (Roman script) to match the question — not Devanagari.

## Dark and light theme
Home screen header has a theme toggle (dark / light). It saves on this device. It does not change Ask reply language or Cosmic Help language.

## Support and website
Support email: supportcosmiclens@gmail.com. Website: https://cosmiclens.app. Cosmic Help is in-app support — not V3 Live and not kundli prediction.

## What Cosmic Help can answer
Cosmic Help answers app how-to for every client screen (Home, Life Map, Ask, Future, More, Profile, payments, My Reports) plus THIS account’s orders and COSMO ID. Personal kundli readings go to the Ask tab. Refunds, chargebacks, and screenshots go to the team in this same chat — wait here.

## Help vs Ask vs Divya Prashna
Cosmic Help (Profile → Help): app how-to, payments, this account. Ask tab V1 / V3 Live: personal chart questions (packs / live minutes). Divya Prashna: instant prashna on that screen only — it does not use Ask pack credits and is not Cosmic Help.

## Onboarding and create kundli
First-time / unlock flows: create primary kundli with name, date of birth, birth time, and place. Without kundli, many Home tools show demo or unlock banners.

## Profile and family
Kundli / birth details: Profile → edit. Add family or partner profiles (name, gender, DOB, time, place). Set one as primary. Delete account: Profile → About → type DELETE.

## Delete account effects
Deleting is permanent: login removed; kundlis, profiles, and chat erased within about 30 days. GST invoices may be kept for legal periods. Confirm by typing DELETE.

## Referral
Refer & Earn: Profile. Share code like CL + your user id. Friend enters once at signup. When that friend buys any Ask V1 or V3 pack, you get 3 free Ask questions (one reward per referred buyer).

## Home features
Home (needs kundli): Today’s Energy, 7-day Energy Forecast, Dosh Analysis, Risk Radar, Lucky Elements, Personalization. Kundli Milan may also be linked from Home.

## Personalization
Home → Personalization (needs kundli). Shows how your kundli understands you: Kundli Category (power type) with VIEW detail, Strongest Trait, Pressure Point, and scored insight cards. Free on-screen — not a PDF. Without kundli, create one first.

## Life Map
Life Map: Relationship, Career, Health, Finance. Explore: Numerology, AstroVastu, Palmistry, Face Reading (coming soon — not on sale yet). Business Vastu from Explore / Business entry. Career from Life Map may require a ₹1 Career Analysis Unlock — see faq knowledge.

## Health screen
Life Map → Health. On-screen wellness / Tridosha-style summary from your saved kundli (energy, sensitivities). Needs a kundli. Free on-screen summary — not a PDF and not a Cosmic Help prediction. If a deeper card is locked, follow that screen (one-time pay if shown). Expert founder PDFs are separate My Reports products.

## Finance screen
Life Map → Finance. On-screen money-flow / wealth-style summary from your saved kundli. Needs a kundli. Free on-screen summary — not a PDF and not Cosmic Help prediction. Deeper cards follow that screen. Expert PDFs are separate one-time My Reports products.

## Ask and Future
Ask: Ask V1 and V3 Live (one-time packs / sessions). A special advanced engine reads your chart and answers — not AI. Future tab: on-screen current dasha timeline (MD / AD / PD style) — not a PDF. Dasha Risk / timing notes on Future are on-screen guidance, not Cosmic Help predictions.

## Kundli and charts
More → Planet Position (titled Kundli): free birth chart / planetary positions — D1, Divisional, KP, Ashtakavarga, Navatara, Jaimini, Transit tabs; planet cards show sign, degrees, house. Kundli tab also shows Ascendant, Moon, Nakshatra, and navigable dasha (MD / Antar / Pratyantar). Needs saved kundli (demo for some tabs without it).

## Panchang
More → Panchang & Muhurat. Panchang Today: Tithi, Nakshatra, Yoga, Karana, Vaar, sunrise/sunset, auspicious block, Tarabala, Brahma Muhurta, Rahu/Gulika/Yamaganda times; also Muhurat / Vrat / Vivah / Jaap tabs as shown.

## Muhurat
Muhurat: auspicious times for Wedding, Griha Pravesh, Business Start, Vehicle, Naming, Mundan, Yagyopavit, Travel. Dates are approximate — confirm with a pandit for ceremonies.

## Gemstones
More → Gemstones: Vedic gemstone guide (Navratna style tips) and certified gems shop. Request on WhatsApp for real photos/videos before paying. In-app buy (if shown) uses the Pay screen amount — confirm there. Paid gemstone orders appear on Help → Transactions. Cosmic Help does not invent a gemstone rupee price.

## Daily Alerts and notifications
Daily Alerts: daily planetary guidance cards (energy score, Moon, insight, lucky colour/numbers). Multi-profile where shown. If alerts do not arrive: phone Settings → Cosmic Lens → Notifications ON, then reopen the app. Push may also cover V3 ready / report ready when enabled.

## Instagram Answers
Instagram Answers (reel / DM auto-reply) is not live. It is hidden in the app. Do not give a how-to. Say it is not available yet.

## My Reports how to open
More → My Library → My Reports (also from order success → Track). Pull down to refresh. Pending means the expert is still preparing it (standard 4–6 business days). Tap a delivered row to open the PDF. Palmistry VIP video is sent on WhatsApp — it is not a My Reports PDF file.

## Talk to Founder
Talk to Founder: Instagram / YouTube / WhatsApp links to discuss kundli or questions personally (not Cosmic Help chat).

## Divya Prashna
Divya Prashna: ask a question for an instant Vedic-style answer in that screen (separate from Ask V1 packs and Cosmic Help).

## Remedies
Remedies: planet-focused mantra, gem, daan, and upay style guidance on that screen.

## Rashifal
Rashifal: daily / weekly / monthly horoscope style reading on that screen — not Cosmic Help.
