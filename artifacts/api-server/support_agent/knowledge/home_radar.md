# Home — Today's Energy, Forecast, Dosh, Lucky, Radar

## What is Today's Energy
Today's Energy (Aaj ki Energy) is a daily score from 1–100 on the Home screen. It shows how supportive today's planetary transits are for you, based on your birth kundli. You need a saved kundli. Tap the Home energy card for the in-app “How it works” screen.

## How Today's Energy works
The score blends two classical Vedic factors: Tara chakra (55%) — compares today's Moon nakshatra with your birth nakshatra; Ashtakavarga (45%) — measures how strong the Moon's current house is in your chart.

## Today's Energy score meaning
75–100 Strong positive energy. 55–74 Moderate, stay focused. 35–54 Unstable, plan carefully. Below 35 Low energy, rest and introspect.

## When Today's Energy updates
The score refreshes when you open the app. It uses today's live Moon transit against your saved kundli.

## 7-day Energy Forecast
Home → 7 Day Energy Forecast. Pick each of the next days: score /100, summary tone (UP / MIXED / DOWN), lucky number, lucky colour, best time, avoid time. Needs kundli for personalized forecast; without kundli you may see a demo unlock banner.

## Dosh Analysis
Home → Dosh Analysis. Shows total / present / not present doshas with Active / Mild / Clear status. Expand a card for remedies. Common doshas listed include Manglik, Kaal Sarp, Pitru, Guru Chandal, Grahan, and others shown on screen. Disclaimer: consult a Jyotishi for major decisions. Needs kundli for personal results.

## Lucky Elements
Lucky Elements screen (from Home energy surfaces): today’s lucky colours, numbers, days, gemstone tip, direction / metal / element, guardian deity, planetary mantra — based on Moon rashi (and Lagna when kundli exists). Not a separate paid product.

## What is Risk Radar
Risk Radar is on the Home tab. It shows cosmic risk signals for the next 24 hours and a 7-day view (HIGH ALERT / ELEVATED / STABLE style badges).

## How Radar works for clients
Clients see sections such as what’s the risk, what to avoid, what to do, remedy, and best / avoid times where shown. Needs a saved kundli. Day 1 of the longer radar view is typically free; days 2–7 may require a paid unlock as shown on screen (one-time / paywall on that screen — not a monthly subscription plan).

## Lucky colour and number on Home
Lucky colour and lucky number for the day also appear via Home radar / energy surfaces — not a separate paid SKU.
