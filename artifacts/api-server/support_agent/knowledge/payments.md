# Payments, refunds, and transactions

## No wallet
Cosmic Lens has NO rupee wallet / balance. Paid orders show on Help → Transactions. Ask question credits show on Profile → Cosmic Packs or the Ask pack bar (“N of 3 free”, “Pack · left/total · till date”). That Ask bar is question credits only — not money.

## How to pay
Prices in ₹ include applicable GST. Pay via UPI / card / netbanking in the payment sheet. If the payment sheet closed, tap Pay again.

## Confirm amount
Always confirm the exact amount on the Pay screen before paying — on-screen checkout is authoritative for that purchase.

## Payment processors (what clients see)
Checkout may show Razorpay or Cashfree depending on the payment screen. Legal / About text often names Razorpay for packs and purchases. If a payment fails, retry on the same Pay screen or contact support with order details.

## Refunds — when granted
Typical refund cases: duplicate charge; paid but feature not activated; technical failure lasting more than about 72 hours; unused-pack activation failure (team discretion). Email supportcosmiclens@gmail.com with order ID. Team aims to reply in about 3 business days; payout often 5–10 business days via the payment partner.

## Refunds — when not granted
Usually not: change of mind after credit or delivery; “prediction felt wrong”; used or expired pack; wrong birth data entered by the client; free signup questions; requests more than about 30 days after payment.

## Failed bank debit
If the bank was debited but payment stayed pending/failed, reverse often happens in about 5–7 business days (bank/RBI timing). Exact bank settlement date is not visible in Help — escalate if needed.

## Chargebacks
Raising a chargeback without contacting support first may lead to account suspension.

## Priority fee guarantee
For products that offer 12-hour Priority: if the 12-hour delivery window is missed, the Priority fee is 100% refunded (as stated on the product screen).

## Packs are one-time
V1 and V3 packs are one-time purchases (not a monthly subscription). Unused questions or live minutes expire as shown on Cosmic Packs.
