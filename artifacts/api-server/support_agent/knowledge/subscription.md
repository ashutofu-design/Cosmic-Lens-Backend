# Purchases — one-time only

## No subscription plans
Cosmic Lens does **not** sell a monthly Basic / Pro subscription plan for Cosmic Help answers. Client purchases are **one-time** (Ask packs, V3 Live minutes, expert PDFs/videos, Vastu uploads, Career ₹1 unlock, Birth Time Rectification, etc.). If someone asks about “subscription cancel / renew / ₹199 month / ₹499 month”, say Cosmic Lens paid products are one-time — confirm what they bought on Help → Transactions or Cosmic Packs.

## What clients buy (one-time)
- Ask V1 question packs (expire as shown on Cosmic Packs)
- V3 Live timed sessions
- Expert Pro PDFs / videos (Numerology, Love Reality, Kundli Milan, Palmistry, Vastu, etc.) → My Reports after delivery
- AstroVastu / Business Vastu paid room or PDF checkouts
- Birth Time Rectification ₹999
- Career Analysis Unlock ₹1 (when Life Map → Career requires it)

## Free vs paid
Many screens are free on-screen (Home energy, Love Reality Basic, Milan Basic, Numerology Basic, Palmistry Basic, Future dasha view, etc.). Deeper expert PDFs and packs are separate one-time pays. Always confirm the amount on the Pay screen.

## “Pro” in product names
“Pro” on Love Reality Pro, Numerology Pro, Palmistry Pro, etc. means a **one-time** expert product — not a monthly subscription plan.

## If they see an old Plans screen
If the app still shows an old Plans / Cashfree subscription UI, tell them paid Cosmic Lens products for support answers are treated as one-time purchases; for that screen’s amount, confirm on Pay or escalate to the team with a screenshot.
