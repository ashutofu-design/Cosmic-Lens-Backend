## AstroVastu — free vs paid
Open: Life Map → Explore → AstroVastu.
Compass / direction guide tools on screen are free.
Always confirm the exact amount on the Pay screen — different AstroVastu products use different price ladders.

Founder/expert photo review upload: about ₹199 per room (SKU style “1 Room · Expert Vastu Review”) — separate from credit packs. Confirm on Pay.

## AstroVastu full home / commercial floor PDF
Full home floor-plan PDF checkout often shows about ₹999 for home. Shop / Office / Factory floor PDF amounts on the AstroVastu product screen may differ from Business Vastu PDF prices and from Plans stickers — quote only what that Pay screen shows.

## Business Vastu checkout (commercial flow)
Open Business Vastu for Shop / Office / Factory.
Room photos (no PDF): Shop ₹399 / Office ₹499 / Factory ₹999 per room.
Full floor-plan PDF: Shop ₹2999 / Office ₹6999 / Factory ₹14999.
PDF mode and room photos are separate — if PDF is selected, rooms are locked and Pay uses the PDF price.
Priority delivery +₹149 when offered on screen.
Do not mix these with AstroVastu marketing stickers (₹999 / ₹1,499 / ₹2,999) unless the client is on that exact Pay screen.

## Palmistry Basic
Open: Life Map → Explore → Palmistry.
Basic is free on-screen palm-line guide (Life / Heart / Head / Fate lines and mounts). Tap cards to expand. No photo required for Basic.

## Palmistry Pro
Pro PDF ₹1499 — both-hands photo palm scan, founder-reviewed PDF to My Reports (standard 4–6 business days).
VIP Video ₹2999 — personal WhatsApp video explanation (no PDF); about 4–6 days.
Priority +₹299 → about 12 hours (PDF → My Reports; video → WhatsApp).
How-to: choose PDF or Video → optional Priority → pay → upload left + right palm photos → pick language for PDF (EN / Hinglish / Hindi).
