**The Big Picture** Teri life me serious relationship ke chances hain, par kuch challenges bhi hain jo relationship ki quality aur stability par asar daal sakte hain. Lekin dikkat yeh hai ki tere relationship wale angle me kuch tough conditions bhi hain. Tera communication style kabhi kabhi complicated hai, jis se misunderstandings ho sakti hain. Saat hi, thoda distance aur delays bhi aate rahenge, matlab relationship me kabhi kabhi thodi doori ya control maintain karne ki zarurat padegi. Kabhi kabhi gusse ya temper se chhoti moti problems ho sakti hain, jinki wajah se relationship me repair karne ki zarurat padti hai. Iska matlab yeh ke tu commitment chahta hai, par uske liye samay aur mehnat dono deni padengi. Emotions thode mixed rahenge, toh lazmi hai ki feelings ko express karne me clearly bane rehna.
